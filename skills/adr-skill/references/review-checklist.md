# ADR Review Checklist

Could a contributor or coding agent understand what was decided and why without reading the original conversation?

## Context

- Is the problem and its trigger clear?
- Are relevant constraints and related decisions explained?
- Can a reader understand the terminology without tribal knowledge?

## Decision

- Is the chosen option explicit, with a reason for choosing it?
- Are scope and non-goals clear?
- Are accepted costs and risks stated alongside the choice?

## Alternatives

- Do the alternatives reflect options that were actually considered?
- Are their benefits, drawbacks, and reasons for rejection clear?
- Are tradeoffs explained once, without a repetitive Consequences section?

## Format and Lifecycle

- Does the filename follow the repository convention?
- Is the status accurate and consistent with the index?
- Are there no unfinished placeholders or unnecessary sections?
- Does the record use a status line instead of YAML frontmatter?
- Are implementation steps and verification kept in linked issues or PRs?
- Are superseding or related ADRs linked where relevant?

Fix material gaps using the available evidence. Ask the human when rationale is missing; do not invent alternatives or pad the record with implementation detail.
